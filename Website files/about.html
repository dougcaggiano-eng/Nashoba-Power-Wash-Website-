About us

Local. Hands-on. Detail-minded.

Nashoba Power Wash is owned and run by Doug in Westford, MA.

Doug started the business in the summer of 2025 and has grown it over the past couple of summers. We're a small, hands-on, locally owned operation, and the owner is involved in every job.

What we love most is the reaction. A lot of people don't realize how big a difference a good power wash makes until they see it. And it's more than looks. The mold and grime that build up on surfaces can lead to them breaking down, which costs more money down the road.

We like to get to know our customers and make sure we're on the same page before we start. If we think a job could damage your property, we'll tell you instead of taking the risk.
