Questions, answered

The things customers ask us most.

Are you taking new customers? Can I get a quote?

Yes. Quotes are free. Send us a request and tell us what you'd like cleaned. Our busy season runs mid-May through August, so the earlier you reach out, the easier it is to get you on the schedule.

Can I schedule my free estimate online?

Yes. Use the schedule page to pick a day and time. Your request comes to us and we'll confirm the time with you. We come to your property, look at what you want cleaned, and give you an accurate price. The estimate is free.

How does pricing work?

Every property is different, so we price each job based on the work involved: the size, the surface, its condition and how hard it is to reach. We don't post prices. We do free estimates in person so the number is accurate. If you send photos, we can give you a price range, and we'll confirm the final price once we see the job in person.

How long does a job take?

Most house washes take about 3 to 4 hours, and composite decks are usually 2 to 3 hours, depending on size. Patio restoration takes 2 to 3 days because the patio has to dry before we put the sand in. Fences depend on the size.

What is patio restoration?

It's our favorite service. We power wash the patio, clean the mold out of the joints between the pavers, let it dry, then re-sand the joints with polymeric sand. The patio looks brand new.

What do I need to have ready?

We need access to a water source at your property. It also helps to be specific about everything you want cleaned. That way we agree on the full job up front.

What if it rains?

We work in dry conditions. A light sprinkle is fine, but we can't work in the rain, so we'd move your job to another day.

Will the cleaning solution hurt my plants, windows or pool?

We take steps to prevent that. We rinse surfaces before we apply the solution and rinse everything thoroughly after, including plants and windows, so nothing is left behind to stain or harm them. We also check where water runs off so the solution doesn't build up. If we're working near a pool, we'll let you know that some dirt may wash into it.

Is there anything you won't clean?

Yes. We stay away from jobs where pressure could do more harm than good, like chewed-up wood decks, oxidized siding, or old siding that hasn't been painted in a while. We're also extra careful around damaged siding. We'd rather tell you honestly than risk damaging your property.

Where do you work?

We serve homeowners in Westford, Chelmsford, Groton, Acton, Tewksbury, Littleton, Lowell, Ayer, Burlington, Concord and Carlisle, and other towns within about 20 miles of Westford. Travel is built into your quote.

How do I pay?

We accept cash, check or Venmo.
