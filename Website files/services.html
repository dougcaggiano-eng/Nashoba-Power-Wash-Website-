Our services

Every surface is different, so we match the method to the material.

Our specialty

Patio restoration

Over time, mold and grime settle into the joints between pavers and dull the whole patio. We power wash the surface, clean the mold out of the joints, let everything dry, then re-sand the joints with polymeric sand. The patio looks brand new.

Plan on 2 to 3 days. The patio has to dry before the sand goes in.

House washing

Dirt, mold and grime build up on vinyl siding and can wear it down over time. We apply a bleach-based cleaning solution from the ground up so it doesn't streak, then rinse from the top down. Plants, windows and surrounding surfaces get rinsed so they're protected.

Most house washes take about 3 to 4 hours, depending on the size of the house.

Composite deck cleaning

Composite decks collect mold and grime that dull the surface and make it slippery. A proper cleaning brings the deck back without harsh pressure.

Usually 2 to 3 hours, depending on the size of the deck.

Fence cleaning

Fences pick up green growth and grime fast. We use the right tools for the job and take extra care around wood, since too much pressure can damage it.

Time depends on the size of the fence.

Patio cleaning

If your patio doesn't need a full restoration, a thorough clean can still make a big difference. For large flat surfaces we use a surface cleaner for an even, streak-free finish.

Mold, grime and other surfaces

Siding and decks are what we clean most, but if another surface needs it, just ask. We'll tell you honestly whether it's a good fit before we start.

How we price

Every property is different, so we don't post prices. We quote each job based on the work involved, and estimates are free and done in person so the number is accurate. If you'd rather send photos, we can give you a price range.

  • Size of the area. A small patio and a large house take different amounts of time.
  • Type of surface. Siding, pavers, composite and fences each need a different method.
  • Condition. Light dirt is quicker than heavy mold or built-up grime.
  • Access. Getting up to high areas takes more time and effort.
  • Distance. Travel is built into your quote.

We accept cash, check or Venmo.

Good to know

  • We need a water source at your property.
  • Weather matters. We work in dry conditions. A light sprinkle is fine, but we can't work in the rain.
  • We'll be upfront about limits. Some surfaces can be damaged by pressure, like worn-out wood decks, oxidized siding or siding that hasn't been painted in a long time. If we think a job could harm your property, we'll tell you rather than risk it.

Tell us exactly what you want cleaned when you request your quote. A clear list up front means no surprises for either of us.
